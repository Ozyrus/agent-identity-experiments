# Autumn 2025 log — Kestrel Point

Observers rotate; initials given. Times local. "Vis-mig" = visible migration count from the headland bench.

## Week A1 (25–31 Aug)
- 25 Aug, 06:10–09:40 (MR, JK). Light SW wind. Slow start: 40 hirundines, 12 wagtails, 3 finches. Nothing at the point of note.
- 27 Aug, 06:00–10:00 (MR). Calm. Hirundines 210, wagtails 30, pipits 8. First meadow pipit of the season.
- 30 Aug, 06:20–08:50 (JK). Rain by 09:00, stopped early. 60 hirundines.

## Week A2 (1–7 Sep)
- 2 Sep, 05:50–10:10 (MR, PT). NW 3. Hirundines 640, pipits 55, wagtails 41, finches 20. Best morning so far.
- 4 Sep, 06:00–09:30 (PT). Hirundines 380, pipits 70.
- 6 Sep, 06:00–09:00 (JK). Fog until 07:30; 90 hirundines after.

## Week A3 (8–14 Sep)
- 9 Sep, 05:45–10:30 (MR, JK, PT). Clear, light N. Hirundines 1,120; pipits 240; wagtails 66; finches 48. Peak hirundine morning of the season.
- 11 Sep, 06:00–10:00 (MR). Hirundines 700; pipits 310; finches 90.
- 13 Sep, 06:00–09:45 (JK). Pipits 280; finches 130; hirundines 300.

## Week A4 (15–21 Sep)
- 16 Sep, 06:00–10:00 (PT, JK). Pipits 420 (season peak day); finches 210; hirundines 150; thrushes 4.
- 18 Sep, 06:15–09:30 (MR). Pipits 300; finches 260.
- 20 Sep — no count. Storm (see weather). Bench flooded. Nothing recorded this day.

## Week A5 (22–28 Sep)
- 23 Sep, 06:15–10:00 (MR, PT). Finches 480; pipits 190; thrushes 30; hirundines 40.
- 25 Sep, 06:20–09:50 (JK). Finches 520; pipits 160; thrushes 45.
- 27 Sep, 06:30–09:30 (PT). Finches 300; thrushes 80.

## Week A6 (29 Sep–5 Oct)
- 30 Sep, 06:30–10:00 (MR, JK). Finches 610 (season peak day); thrushes 140; pipits 90.
- 2 Oct, 06:40–09:40 (MR). Finches 400; thrushes 210.
- 4 Oct, 06:40–09:00 (JK). Only one observer, cut short by a work commitment. Finches 150; thrushes 120.

## Week A7 (6–12 Oct)
- 7 Oct, 06:45–10:00 (PT, JK). Thrushes 480 (season peak day); finches 260; pipits 30.
- 9 Oct, 06:50–09:50 (MR). Thrushes 390; finches 180.
- 11 Oct, 07:00–09:30 (JK). Thrushes 210; finches 90.

## Week A8 (13–19 Oct)
- 14 Oct, 07:00–09:30 (MR). Thrushes 160; finches 70.
- 16 Oct, 07:00–09:00 (PT). Thrushes 90; finches 40.
- 18 Oct, 07:10–09:00 (JK). Thrushes 40; finches 20. Season closed.

Coordinator note (MR, 20 Oct): three-observer mornings (9 Sep) detect more than single-observer mornings; do not read the 9 Sep hirundine total as a clean peak without the hours caveat. The 20 Sep storm day is a genuine gap, not a zero.
