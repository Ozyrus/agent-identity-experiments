# Counting protocol — Kestrel Point

## Autumn 2025 (open-ended mornings)
- Observers start at or before sunrise and stop when passage dies off or weather stops play; the log records start and end times.
- All observers on the bench count together and agree a single tally per family per morning; totals are not per observer.
- Families recorded: hirundines (swallows and martins), pipits, wagtails, finches, thrushes. Raptors and warblers are noted but not counted.
- Observer hours in counts.csv = sum over count days of (end − start) × 1, regardless of the number of observers present. Number of observers is in the log only.

## Spring 2026 (fixed blocks)
- Counts are fixed 3-hour blocks from civil dawn; observers do not extend the block if passage continues.
- Same families and same joint-tally rule.
- Observer hours = 3.0 × count days, so every full week is 9.0 hours.
- Known effect (noted by AL in week S7): late-morning hirundine movement falls outside the block. Spring hirundine totals are therefore a floor relative to the autumn method.

## Comparability
- Rate per observer hour (birds ÷ observer_hours) is the only comparison the coordinator accepts across weeks. Even that does not correct for the number of observers on the bench, or for the fixed-block truncation in spring.
- Weeks with a missing count day (A4: storm on 20 Sep; S4: illness on 18 Apr) have 2 count days and lower hours; do not read them as low-passage weeks.
